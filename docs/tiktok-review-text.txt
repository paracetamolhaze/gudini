Gudini (https://gudinijr.duckdns.org) is a web studio for creators: it turns a recorded talking-head video into a finished short with subtitles, a cover and a caption, and lets the creator publish it to their own TikTok account.

Login Kit (user.info.basic): the creator connects their own TikTok account via OAuth on the Settings page. We use user.info.basic with creator_info to show the creator's nickname and avatar on the publish screen, so they see which account the video goes to.

Content Posting API, Direct Post (video.publish): on the publish screen the creator previews the video, edits the caption, selects the privacy level from the options returned by creator_info (nothing is preselected), sets comment/duet/stitch permissions, picks the cover frame, declares commercial content (your brand / branded content) and confirms the Music Usage Confirmation (and Branded Content Policy). Only then the video is uploaded (FILE_UPLOAD) and posted; we poll publish status and show the result. No other TikTok data is read or stored.
